In 1639 a commoner named Eskild Andersen Kongsbakke sailed for Denmark's only fort in India: Dansborg, at Tranquebar (today's Tharangambadi, Tamil Nadu). Then the ships stopped coming. The company went bankrupt, the governor died, and the soldiers chose a quartermaster who could barely read. With the Nayak of Tanjore's army at the walls, no money and no orders, he held on by trading, privateering and outlasting a nine-month siege. When a Danish frigate finally arrived in 1669, its officers carried orders for what to do if he was dead.
